About Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj

Nestled in the majestic Dhauladhar mountain range, Dharamshala and its upper suburb McLeod Ganj offer a stunning blend of natural beauty and deep spiritual heritage. Known globally as "Little Lhasa,"
it is the residence of His Holiness the Dalai Lama and the heart of the exiled Tibetan community in India. The region offers much more than just mountain views. With a culture heavily influenced by Tibetan traditions,
the towns boast vibrant monasteries, colorful prayer flags, and a deeply peaceful atmosphere. Whether you are looking for thrilling Himalayan treks, serene meditation retreats,
exploring ancient Hindu temples, or a culinary journey through authentic momos and thukpa, this region provides a deeply enriching and unforgettable mountain escape.

Hidden Spots to Explore (Other than main temples)

While the main squares of McLeod Ganj are legendary, the true essence of the mountains often lies hidden in the quieter trails. Escape the crowds and explore these offbeat locations:

  • Kareri Lake: A pristine, high-altitude, shallow fresh-water lake situated to the south of the Dhauladhar range. The trek takes you through lush oak and rhododendron forests, offering spectacular, untouched views.

  • Naddi View Point: Located away from the bustling town, this serene sunset point offers panoramic, unobstructed views of the snow-capped Dhauladhar ranges on one side and the lush Kangra valley on the other.

  • Masroor Rock Cut Temples: Step back in time to the 8th century at this stunning archaeological site. These monolithic rock-cut temples, carved out of a single rock, offer a peaceful historical escape.

  • Norbulingka Institute: Dedicated to preserving Tibetan culture in its purest form. Wander through its beautiful Japanese-style gardens, watch artisans craft traditional thangkas, and enjoy the tranquil environment.
